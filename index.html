Independent project - 2026

Skin Cancer Classification via Fine-Tuned CNN

Early detection is critical in dermatology, yet distinguishing malignant from benign skin lesions from images alone is a hard visual classification problem. I fine-tuned a MobileNetV2 model to classify skin lesion images as malignant or benign, reaching a 90.7% validation score and a 95.85% validation auxiliary score.

The project covered the full deep learning workflow: preprocessing and augmenting the image dataset, fine-tuning the pretrained MobileNetV2 architecture, tuning hyperparameters to improve generalization, and validating performance to avoid overfitting on a medically sensitive task.

Team project - Sept 2025 – Jan 2026

Medleaf - Conversational RAG Agent for Drug Leaflet Understanding

Medication leaflets are often complex and highly technical, which can lead to critical misunderstandings regarding dosage, side effects, and contraindications. To bridge this gap, I developed a RAG-based (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) conversational chatbot that provides clear, simplified, and reliable answers from trusted medical sources.

The system pulls official drug information from the FDA Open Data sources and can also process documents uploaded by the user. The retrieved text is cleaned, chunked, embedded, and stored in a persistent vector database, enabling fast semantic retrieval during inference and allowing the chatbot to answer questions grounded in the most relevant medical content.

Team project - 2023 – 2025

Dashboard for Educational Equality

As a team of five, we aimed to solve a concrete challenge: the sheer volume and complexity of available national education data. Our goal was to make this information clear, intuitive, and accessible to non-data specialists, in order to facilitate strategic decision-making for stakeholders within the Ministry of National Education.

To achieve this, we extracted and cleaned the national educational datasets using Python (Pandas), and built advanced clustering models, including K-Means and DBSCAN, followed by hyperparameter optimization. In parallel, we developed an interactive dashboard to visualize our clustering results seamlessly. Finally, our project was presented to a panel of jurors to validate our work.

My role: Data extraction and cleaning (Python), model development (Python).
