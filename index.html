Independent project - 2026

Skin Cancer Classification via Fine-Tuned CNN

Early detection is critical in dermatology, yet distinguishing malignant from benign skin lesions from images alone is a hard visual classification problem. I fine-tuned a MobileNetV2 model to classify skin lesion images as malignant or benign, reaching 87.9% validation accuracy and a 0.95 validation AUC.

The project covered transfer learning with MobileNetV2 followed by fine-tuning, data augmentation, and early stopping to improve generalization and avoid overfitting on a medically sensitive task.

Team project - Jun – Sept 2026

Medleaf - Conversational RAG Agent for Drug Leaflet Understanding

Medication leaflets are often complex and highly technical, which can lead to critical misunderstandings regarding dosage, side effects, and contraindications. To bridge this gap, I developed a RAG-based (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) conversational chatbot that provides clear, simplified, and reliable answers from trusted medical sources.

The assistant is built on official FDA drug documents stored in a ChromaDB vector memory, and runs on a local open-source LLM (Ollama) chosen after a cost / capability analysis. Users can also upload their own documents (PDF, TXT). The app is containerized with Docker and comes with a Streamlit interface.

To measure reliability I used DeepEval, analysed the causes of errors (chunking, hallucinations) and improved the prompt engineering, raising the average score from 0.5 to 0.83.

As a team of five, we aimed to solve a concrete challenge: the sheer volume and complexity of available national education data. Our goal was to make this information clear, intuitive, and accessible to non-data specialists, in order to facilitate strategic decision-making for stakeholders within the Ministry of National Education.
